Microsoft explanation

This policy setting allows you to block the synchronization of contacts between Outlook and the Global Address List (GAL).

If you enable this policy setting, GAL contact synchronization is blocked.

If you disable or you do not configure this policy setting, GAL contact synchronization is allowed.
