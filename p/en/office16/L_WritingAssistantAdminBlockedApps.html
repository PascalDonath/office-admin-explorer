Cloud Policy

Cloud Policy (config.office.com → Customization → Policy Management) offers many user policies of the Microsoft 365 Apps – search for Apps blocked by Writing Assistance admin policy. It also applies to unmanaged devices and wins over GPO and Intune for the same setting.

Microsoft explanation

This policy setting allows administrators to specify a list of applications where Writing Assistance will be blocked.

If you enable this policy setting, enter one application executable name per line (e.g., notepad.exe). Writing Assistance will be disabled in those applications.

If you disable or do not configure this policy setting, Writing Assistance will not be blocked by this policy in any application.
