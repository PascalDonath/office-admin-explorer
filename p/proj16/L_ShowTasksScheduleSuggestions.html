Microsoft explanation

This policy setting will allow the display of green task suggestions to indicate potential optimization.

If you enable this policy setting, a green task suggestion will be displayed to indicate potential problems.

If you disable or do not configure this policy setting, a green task suggestion will not be displayed to indicate potential problems.
